# AshBak Industries Brand Identity Kit

A complete brand identity kit for **AshBak Industries Inc.** — the industrial backbone of Africa — providing integrated infrastructure for commerce, payments, and logistics across African markets.

## What's included

- **Brand System** (`/brand-system`) — Logo, colour, typography, spacing, governance sheets
- **Letterhead** (`/letterhead`) — A4 letterheads, envelopes, with-compliments
- **Email** (`/email`) — Signatures and newsletter header system
- **Presentation** (`/presentation`) — 16:9 master template
- **Commercial Documents** (`/commercial`) — Quotation, proforma, invoice, order confirmation
- **Business Card** (`/business-card`) — 3.5 × 2 in duplex sets
- **Document Covers** (`/document-covers`) — A4 cover layouts
- **Catalogue** (`/catalogue`) — Category cover, grid, product, spec, comparison
- **Reports** (`/reports`) — Cover, executive summary, data, charts, conclusions
- **Digital & Social** (`/digital`) — Covers, profiles, highlights, LinkedIn posts, safe guides
- **Assets** (`/assets`) — Favicons, app icons, icon sheet, patterns, QR, download packs

## Brand tokens

### Logo
- **Text-only wordmark**: lowercase `ashbak` in Inter Tight Medium (500)
- **No separate icon mark** — the wordmark is the primary logo for all applications
- **Clear space**: equal to the height of the lowercase `a` on all sides
- **Minimum size**: 14 px on screen / 12 mm in print

### Colours

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#000000` | Main backgrounds, wordmark on light |
| Secondary | `#FFFFFF` | Main text on dark, reversed wordmark |
| Dark Gray | `#111112` | Secondary backgrounds |
| Light Gray | `#F4F3F9` | Secondary text, background elements |

### Typography
- **Headings**: Inter Tight Medium (500)
- **Body**: Inter Tight Regular (400) or system fonts
- **Display / Hero**: Inter Tight Bold (700) with tight tracking

### Company info
- **Name**: AshBak Industries
- **Legal name**: AshBak Industries Inc.
- **Positioning**: The Industrial Backbone of Africa
- **Descriptor**: Integrated infrastructure for commerce, payments, and logistics across African markets
- **Tagline**: Building the Industrial Backbone of Africa
- **Website**: ashbakindustries.com
- **Email**: hello@ashbakindustries.com
- **LinkedIn**: linkedin.com/company/ashbak

### Social platforms
Only **LinkedIn** is included (per brand requirements). No Facebook, X, Instagram, Google, or other platforms.

## Design aesthetic
- Minimalist and clean
- Dark mode aesthetic with white text (black backgrounds, white typography)
- Professional and industrial feel
- Clear visual hierarchy with ample white space

## Sub-brands
AshBak Industries operates three integrated business layers:
1. **NubiaGo** — Commerce
2. **Payment Infrastructure** — Payments
3. **Bagster** — Logistics
