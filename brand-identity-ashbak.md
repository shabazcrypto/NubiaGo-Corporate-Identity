# AshBak Industries Brand Identity

## Brand Overview
AshBak Industries is the industrial backbone of Africa, providing integrated infrastructure for commerce, payments, and logistics across African markets. The brand identity reflects professionalism, reliability, and connectivity.

## Logo

**Primary Logo:**
- Text: "ashbak" in lowercase sans-serif font
- Color: White text on dark background (black or very dark gray)
- Style: Clean, modern, minimalist
- Usage: Primary logo for all brand communications

**Secondary Logo:**
- Full name: "AshBak Industries" 
- Color: White text on dark background
- Style: Clean, professional typography

**Logo Usage:**
- Maintain clear space around logo (minimum 1x logo width on all sides)
- Do not alter colors or proportions
- Do not rotate or distort the logo
- Always use the primary logo for primary applications

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- **Black**: #000000 (Primary background)
- **White**: #FFFFFF (Primary text on dark backgrounds)

**Secondary Colors:**
- **Dark Gray**: #111112 (Secondary background)
- **Light Gray**: #F4F3F9 (Secondary text/background elements)

**Color Usage:**
- Primary backgrounds: Black (#000000)
- Primary text on dark: White (#FFFFFF)
- Secondary backgrounds: Dark Gray (#111112)
- Secondary text/background: Light Gray (#F4F3F9)

## Typography

**Primary Font:**
- **Inter Tight** (Medium weight for headings, Regular for body)
- Clean, modern sans-serif typeface

**Secondary Font:**
- System fonts (sans-serif) for body text

**Typography Usage:**
- Headings: Inter Tight Medium (500)
- Body text: Inter Tight Regular (400) or system fonts
- Maintain consistent line height and spacing

## Design Principles

**Aesthetic:**
- Minimalist and clean design
- Dark mode aesthetic with white text
- Professional and industrial feel
- Clear visual hierarchy

**Usage Guidelines:**
- Maintain consistent spacing and alignment
- Use ample white space
- Prioritize readability
- Keep designs simple and focused

## Brand Elements

**Logo Clear Space:**
- Minimum clear space: 1x logo width on all sides
- No other elements should intrude into this space

**Logo Placement:**
- Primary logo: Top left of header
- Secondary logo: Top right of header or as needed

**Color Application:**
- Primary backgrounds: Black (#000000)
- Primary text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Secondary backgrounds: Dark Gray (#111112)
- Secondary text: Light Gray (#F4F3F9)

## Implementation Notes

**For Web:**
- Use CSS variables for consistent color application
- Implement dark mode as default with light mode as alternative
- Ensure proper contrast ratios for accessibility

**For Print:**
- Use Pantone equivalents for print materials
- Maintain minimum logo size of 0.5 inches width in print

**Do Not:**
- Use logo on busy or complex backgrounds
- Add effects (shadows, gradients) to logo
- Alter logo proportions or colors
- Place logo in positions that compromise clear space
